# 02_design — methodology, UX, and voice pass

One job: turn the requirements into a concrete design spec — the math, the exact fields and copy, the result experience — before any building starts.

## Inputs
- Working (this run): ../01_objectives/output/requirements.md
- Reference (every run): ../../_shared/voice.md
- Reference (every run): ../../_shared/brand-guidelines.md

Do NOT load: `_shared/source/marcus-client-brief.md` directly — requirements.md is the distilled version; go back to the brief only if requirements.md is ambiguous on a specific point.

## Process
1. Pick the methodology (25x rule by default) and write out the exact formula, in plain terms, the way it will be explained in the final writeup.
2. Design the input form: final field list and order (within the field cap), and the exact label/copy for each field in Marcus's voice.
3. Design the result experience: what "on track," "behind," and "ahead" each look like, and — critical per brand-guidelines.md — what the path-forward options look like when someone's behind (extra $/month, retire later, work longer at current rate).
4. Voice-pass every piece of copy against voice.md: would Marcus say this out loud?

## Outputs
- `design-spec.md` → output/

## Human check
Read the field list and the sample result copy aloud. Does it sound like Marcus, not a bank calculator? Does "behind" show a path, not just a number?
